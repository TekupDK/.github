# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Tekup Portfolio community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Giving and gracefully accepting** constructive feedback
- **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- **Showing empathy** towards other community members
- **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
- **Being supportive** of fellow contributors and maintainers
- **Respecting time zones** and response time differences
- **Providing context** when asking questions or reporting issues
- **Celebrating others' successes** and contributions

### ❌ Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Dismissing or attacking inclusion-oriented requests or concerns
- Making assumptions about someone's identity, background, or intentions
- Deliberately misgendering people or using 'dead' or rejected names
- Sustained disruption of discussions or community activities

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include:

- Using an official project e-mail address
- Posting via an official social media account
- Acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event
- Participating in project-related discussions, issues, or pull requests
- Contributing code, documentation, or other content to the project

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at:

- **Email**: conduct@tekup.dk
- **GitHub**: Create a confidential security advisory
- **Direct Contact**: Reach out to project maintainers privately

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### What to Include in a Report

When reporting an incident, please include:

- **Your contact information** (so we can follow up with you)
- **Names of any individuals involved** (or if there are additional witnesses)
- **Your account of what occurred** (including if you believe the incident is ongoing)
- **Any additional context** or screenshots that may be helpful
- **Any other information** you believe we should have

### Confidentiality

All reports will be handled with discretion. We will not share your identity or the details of your report without your explicit permission, except as required to protect the safety of others or comply with legal obligations.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## Our Responsibilities as Community Members

### For All Community Members

- **Be respectful** in all interactions
- **Be patient** with others learning and growing
- **Be constructive** with feedback and criticism
- **Be inclusive** and welcoming to newcomers
- **Be professional** in all communications
- **Be mindful** of your language and its impact

### For Contributors

- **Follow the contribution guidelines**
- **Write clear commit messages** and PR descriptions
- **Respond to feedback** graciously and professionally
- **Help maintain code quality** standards
- **Document your work** appropriately
- **Test your changes** thoroughly before submitting

### For Reviewers

- **Provide constructive feedback**
- **Explain the reasoning** behind requested changes
- **Be encouraging** and supportive
- **Focus on the code**, not the person
- **Acknowledge good work** and improvements
- **Be timely** with reviews when possible

### For Maintainers

- **Be responsive** to issues and PRs
- **Communicate clearly** about project direction
- **Recognize and appreciate** contributions
- **Enforce standards** fairly and consistently
- **Lead by example** in behavior and code quality
- **Foster an inclusive** community environment

## Diversity and Inclusion

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all. We welcome and encourage participation from people of all backgrounds and identities.

### Our Commitment

- We actively seek diverse perspectives and voices
- We recognize and value different approaches to problem-solving
- We create space for underrepresented groups in tech
- We accommodate different communication styles and needs
- We respect cultural differences and perspectives

- Problems with color contrast or visual design

## Best Practices for a Healthy Community

### Communication

**✅ DO:**
- Use clear and professional language
- Provide context and background information
- Ask questions when you need clarification
- Acknowledge others' contributions
- Be patient with language barriers
- Use appropriate channels for different types of communication

**❌ DON'T:**
- Use offensive or exclusionary language
- Make assumptions about others' knowledge or experience
- Demand immediate responses
- Go off-topic in discussions
- Cross-post the same question multiple times
- Use all caps or aggressive punctuation (!!!)

### Disagreements

**✅ DO:**
- Focus on the issue, not the person
- Provide evidence and examples
- Consider other perspectives
- Seek to understand before being understood
- Agree to disagree when necessary
- Escalate to maintainers if needed

**❌ DON'T:**
- Make personal attacks
- Use inflammatory language
- Bring up past conflicts
- Take disagreements personally
- Continue arguing in circles
- Harass or intimidate others

### Collaboration

**✅ DO:**
- Give credit where credit is due
- Share knowledge and help others learn
- Coordinate work to avoid duplication
- Communicate your plans and progress
- Ask for help when you need it
- Offer help when you can provide it

**❌ DON'T:**
- Take credit for others' work
- Hoard knowledge or information
- Work in isolation without communication
- Ignore related ongoing work
- Make others feel bad for asking questions
- Overwhelm newcomers with too much information

## Conflict Resolution

### Steps for Resolving Conflicts

1. **Direct Communication**: Try to resolve the issue directly with the other party
2. **Cool Down Period**: Take time to calm down if emotions are high
3. **Seek Mediation**: Ask a neutral maintainer to help mediate
4. **Formal Report**: If unresolved, file a formal report following the enforcement process
5. **Follow Up**: Maintainers will follow up on the resolution

### For Maintainers

When conflicts arise:

1. **Listen to all parties** involved
2. **Gather all relevant information** and context
3. **Review against Code of Conduct** standards
4. **Determine appropriate action** based on severity
5. **Communicate decisions** clearly to all parties
6. **Document the incident** and resolution
7. **Follow up** to ensure resolution is effective
